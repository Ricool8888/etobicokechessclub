Privacy Policy

How the Etobicoke Chess Club handles information on this website.

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Version 1

Overview

The Etobicoke Chess Club (“we”, “us”) is a non-profit community chess club that meets in Etobicoke, Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what information is collected through this website, how it is used, and how you can contact us about it.

1. Information the club itself collects

This website has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics tracking. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

  • Email correspondence. If you email us to ask a question, join the mailing list, or request a photo removal, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply to you and, if you asked to join the mailing list, to send club updates. You can unsubscribe at any time by emailing us, and we act on removal requests promptly.
  • Your theme choice. The light/dark switch stores your preference in your own browser (localStorage) so the site remembers it next visit. It never leaves your device and we have no access to it. The calendar’s month/list view preference is stored the same way.

2. Website hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub automatically logs certain technical information — including visitors’ IP addresses — for security and operational purposes. Those logs are controlled by GitHub, not by the club; we do not receive or review them. GitHub’s handling of this data is described in GitHub’s own privacy statement.

3. Photographs

Our gallery and news pages may include photos taken at club meetings, tournaments, and events, for club records, this website, and our social media.

  • By attending or taking part in a club meeting, tournament, or event, you consent to the use of your image, or your child’s image, for these purposes.
  • We avoid publishing full names or other identifying details alongside photos.
  • If a photo of you (or your child) appears on this site and you would like it removed or blurred, email us at the address below and we will take care of it promptly. You do not need to explain why.

4. Links and embedded content

This site links to services such as Facebook, Instagram, Chess.com, Lichess.org, chess-results.com, Google Maps, the CFC and FIDE. When you follow a link, you leave our site and are governed by that service’s own terms and privacy practices, which we do not control. We encourage you to review their policies.

A few parts of the home page can show content hosted by other services. None of them load until you ask for them:

  • Puzzle of the Day comes from Lichess.org: the puzzle itself is fetched from Lichess’s public API, and the board it is played on is drawn by two open-source chess libraries (chessground and chess.js) that this site serves from its own hosting. Lichess may record your IP address and may set cookies, under its own policy.
  • Live games stream a game from Lichess.org, but only after you press Watch live games. Until then nothing is sent to Lichess for that panel, and the button itself is a link to lichess.org.
  • The meeting map comes from Google Maps, but it does not load until you press Show map. The full street address is printed above the map, so you never have to open one.
  • The engine evaluation bar is switched off by default and only downloads its chess engine from cdnjs if you press Show evaluation.
  • Tournament results are published on chess-results.com, the service we use to run rated tournaments. Following a results link takes you to their site, which handles the request and any cookies under its own privacy policy.

If you would rather not be seen by those services, you can read the news, gallery, tournaments, and FAQ pages without visiting the home page, and everything else on the site is served from our own hosting.

5. Your choices and requests

You may ask us at any time to:

  • remove your email address from our mailing list;
  • tell you what information we hold about you, and correct or delete it; or
  • remove or blur a photo of you or your child.

Just email us and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a future change involves collecting information in a significantly new way, we will explain that on the site before doing so.

7. Contact us

For any privacy question, request, or concern, contact us at etobicokechessclub@gmail.com.
