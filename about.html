About Our Club

Volunteer-run, weekly community chess club. A safe space for all to play at Second Cup, 265 Wincott Dr, Etobicoke, M9A 2T2, Friday 6-9pm. Players of all skill levels are welcome, even those who've never played. Admission and membership is free.

Mandate

Our mandate is to build a chess community in Etobicoke, where people of all ages, skill levels, and backgrounds can discover, enjoy, learn, and grow through the game of chess.

Vision Statement

The Etobicoke Chess Club is a multigenerational, fee-less, volunteer-run Toronto-based Canadian Non-profit which aims to propagate and perpetuate financial and logistical accessibility of the sport of chess for the city of Toronto, indefinitely. Through our casual drop-in chess nights, our youth summer programs held under collaboration with local chess professionals, and our donation-only and low-cost entry tournaments, we aim to reshape and redefine the financial feasibility and accessibility within the city of Toronto for a military strategy game that has been played by people from all walks of life, for about 1500 years. Chess has a history, and it has a presence in today's community. We aim to be part of the history of chess in Toronto, by being part of its future.
